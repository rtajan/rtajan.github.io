# Research

My research lies at the intersection of coding theory and digital communication systems.

## Research interests

- **Polar codes and pre-transformed polar codes.** Distance properties and low-weight codeword
  enumeration (including punctured, shortened and PAC codes), code design and decoding
  algorithms such as list decoding.
- **Faster-than-Nyquist (FTN) signaling.** Transmitter design and iterative receivers based on
  Expectation Propagation, to push data rates closer to theoretical limits.
- **Software for digital communications.** Fast and flexible software implementations of
  encoders and decoders, and software-defined radio on multicore CPUs.

## Projects

- [**AFF3CT**](https://aff3ct.github.io) — *A Fast Forward Error Correction Toolbox*: an
  open-source C++ library (MIT license) for the simulation of forward error correction
  codes and digital communication chains.
- **StreamPU** — a domain-specific embedded language for high-throughput, low-latency
  software-defined radio on multicore CPUs, developed alongside AFF3CT.
- [**AI4CODE**](https://ai4code.projects.labsticc.fr/) — a collaborative project between
  five French laboratories on the design and decoding of forward error correction codes.
