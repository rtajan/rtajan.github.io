## Research interests

<div class="cards">
<div class="card">
<h3>Polar codes</h3>
<p>Distance properties of pre-transformed and PAC codes, code design and list decoding.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3>Faster-than-Nyquist</h3>
<p>Transmitter design and iterative receivers based on Expectation Propagation.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3>Software radio</h3>
<p>Fast encoders and decoders, and software-defined radio on multicore CPUs, with AFF3CT.</p>
</div>
</div>

## About

Since September 2014, I have been an associate professor at
[ENSEIRB-MATMECA](https://enseirb-matmeca.bordeaux-inp.fr) (Bordeaux INP) and a member of the
Signal and Image group of the [IMS Laboratory](https://www.ims-bordeaux.fr) in Bordeaux, France.

From January to August 2014, I was a post-doctoral fellow at CNES in Toulouse, under the
supervision of Charly Poulliat, Marie-Laure Boucheret and Guy Lesthievent.

I received my Ph.D. in December 2013 from the University of Cergy-Pontoise, where I was a member
of the ICI research group of the ETIS laboratory.
